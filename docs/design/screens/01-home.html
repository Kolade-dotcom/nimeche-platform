What membership actually gets you

Three things, and none of them is a group chat.

Certificates that verify

Finish a programme and the certificate arrives. It carries a code, and anyone holding that code can confirm it in one tap without making an account.

A skill record you did not fill in

Attend a CAD clinic, work on a build, present at a review. The platform works out what you have practised and keeps the record for you.

Internships and competitions

Openings from partner firms and the NSE Oyo branch, posted here first, filtered to your department and level.

How it works

1

4
