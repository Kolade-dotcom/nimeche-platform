Campus Solar Dryer

A forced-convection solar dryer for the agriculture department, sized for 20kg of cassava a day. Six members, two airflow redesigns, and one collector that warped in the first week of testing.

Build week

3:07
All 42 photos and the build video

What went wrong, and what we did

The first collector used a 0.5mm mild steel absorber plate, which warped badly on the third day of testing and lost contact with the ducting. We moved to a corrugated sheet with an expansion gap at one end. Airflow dropped about 12 percent, so the second redesign raised the chimney by 400mm to get it back.
