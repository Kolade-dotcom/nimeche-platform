Why

One superfamily doing three jobs. The serif gives headlines the weight of an institution rather than a startup; the sans is quiet enough to disappear in dense admin screens; and the mono is genuinely useful in a product full of verification codes, matric numbers and member numbers. Drawn for IBM, so its whole heritage is engineering.

What it costs

Three faces to load rather than two, though they share a skeleton so the page never looks stitched together. The serif needs a real size to breathe — it earns its place on headlines, not on 14px card titles.
