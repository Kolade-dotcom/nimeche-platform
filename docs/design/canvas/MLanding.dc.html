What NiMechE-SF is

We are the Tech-U student branch of the Nigerian Institution of Mechanical Engineers — the mechanical division of the Nigerian Society of Engineers. The branch runs webinars, workshops, plant visits, competitions and projects for Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering students at Abiola Ajimobi Technical University.

This platform exists so that taking part adds up to something. Attend, complete, earn a certificate an employer can verify — without keeping track of any of it yourself.

What you get

Certificates that verify

Finish a programme and the certificate arrives on its own, carrying a code anyone can check online.

A skill record that is honest

Skills build from what you actually did, across four levels. One webinar never reads as proven competence.

Internships and competitions

SIWES placements, scholarships and design competitions in one place, sorted by deadline instead of scattered across group chats.
