What NiMechE-SF is

We are the Tech-U student branch of the Nigerian Institution of Mechanical Engineers — the mechanical division of the Nigerian Society of Engineers. The branch runs webinars, workshops, plant visits, competitions and projects for Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering students at Abiola Ajimobi Technical University.

This platform exists so that taking part in them adds up to something. Attend, complete, earn a certificate that an employer can verify — without you having to keep track of any of it yourself.

What you get as a member

Certificates that verify

Finish a programme and the certificate arrives on its own. Each one carries a code anyone can check online — no account needed on their side.

A skill record that is honest

Skills build from what you actually did, across four levels. Attending one webinar never reads as proven competence, which is what makes the record worth showing.

Internships and competitions

SIWES placements, scholarships, design competitions and graduate roles, collected in one place and sorted by deadline instead of scattered across group chats.

How it works

1
