## Message 1 — the welcome

```
*Welcome, and thank you for saying yes.* 👋

This group is for the people helping build the NiMechE-SF digital platform. Small on purpose — this is where the work gets decided, not announced.

*What we're building*

Right now everything the branch does happens, ends, and leaves almost no trace. Attendance in a notebook. Certificates as PDFs in a WhatsApp thread. Nobody can check whether any of it is real.

So: a platform where everything you do through NiMechE-SF becomes a professional record you can show an employer — and keep after you graduate. You attend things; the certificates, the skills and the history build themselves.

*Where we are*

We started with design rather than code, on purpose. The public site, joining, the member dashboard and the executive dashboard are all drawn — desktop and phone, light and dark. That part is mostly done.

Next is building the beta. *The goal is to have it finished before we resume school* — not the whole platform, but something real enough to run a live branch event on in the first weeks of the session.

*Two things coming right after this*

1. A short brief — what we're building, the stages, and what's deliberately not in the beta. No dates, no technical detail. Four pages.
2. A link to the designs, so you can see everything and tell us what's wrong with them.

*What I need from you first*

Just the second one. Look at the designs and tell me what doesn't work — confusing wording, a missing screen, something that wouldn't survive a real event. All of it is far cheaper to fix now than after it's built.

Introduce yourself when you get a minute: your level, and what you'd like to touch — building, design, photos and write-ups, testing, or just opinions. All of those are real jobs here.
```

## A note on the link

The designs are currently a **Claude Design canvas**, not a Figma file:

https://claude.ai/code/artifact/c1157cd8-4303-4cbd-820c-f8c9846f3377

It opens in a browser with no account or install, pans and zooms like Figma, and has all 81 screens
laid out by section. It is **private until you share it** from the share menu on the page.

If you specifically want Figma — for comments pinned to screens, or because the team already lives
there — the screens would need rebuilding there; nothing exports automatically. Worth deciding
before you send the link, so the group only gets one.
